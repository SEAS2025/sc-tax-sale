# 2026 delinquent-tax-sale advertisements

Checked **16 September 2026** from official treasurer / tax-collector pages and their linked schedule PDFs. This is a calendar of **sale dates and ad outlets**, not a parcel list. Owner names, addresses, and listing files stay out of git.

The live board is `site/data/ads.json`, produced by `node engine/cli.js scan --write`. Seed dates live in `engine/ads-calendar.js`. The daily scan (GitHub Action or `scripts/scan-ads.sh`) re-reads every official page. After a county’s `watchFrom` / list-promised / first ad date the row stays **due** for seven days so a late posting is still caught, then flips if a 2026 listing href appears.

The same scan rebuilds the statewide 5-year file (`site/data/repeat.json`): parcel IDs on the newest hosted list and on a 2020–2022 archive. New listing URLs, including Florence’s public S3 prefix, are picked up automatically. Identifiers only.

S.C. Code Ann. § 12-51-40 lets each county set its own sale date, then requires the delinquent-tax officer to advertise under “Delinquent Tax Sale” once a week for **three consecutive weeks**. Many 2026 newspaper ads have not run yet. Do not treat a leftover 2025 PDF or HTML table as this year’s universe.

Do not use these lists for commercial solicitation (§ 30-2-50). Tax Sale Atlas and other aggregators are not cited as primary.
